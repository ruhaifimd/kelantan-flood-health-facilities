Flood union is computed on a ~22 m grid. geoBoundaries has 11 Kelantan ADM2 units; MoH lists 10 districts (no separate Kecil Lojing).

Method

  1. Keep Kelantan rows; apply the documented overrides (scripts/overrides.csv; the source CSV is not edited); drop any point not covered by the Kelantan boundary.
  2. Inside extent: the facility's pixel is flooded in the union raster.
  3. Distance: metres to the nearest flooded pixel centre, in GDM2000 / Peninsula RSO (EPSG:3375); 0 when inside.
  4. Within 500 m: distance ≤ 500 m (includes inside).
  5. District counts and percentages use the MoH DAERAH field. Code: scripts/.

Validation against official MOH list

Klinik Kesihatan in the dataset were cross-matched by name and district against the MOH official Klinik Kesihatan list for Kelantan (page last updated 28 Oct 2025; checked 17 Oct 2026).

  • 114 official KK; 107 match exactly by name, all in the same district (including KK Pintu Geng, so it exists; only its source coordinate was wrong).
  • Renamed to the official name: KK Beris Kubor Besar → KK Beris; KK Perol → KK Cabang 3 Perol.
  • KK Baroh Pial and KK Bukit Gading: moh.gov.my lists these two as Klinik Desa; retained as Klinik Kesihatan per local verification by the author (MOH medical officer).
  • Unit Terapi Cara Kerja Ketereh is labelled Klinik Kesihatan in the source; retyped as Other / unit (see overrides).
  • Known data gap: 4 official KK are missing from the MOH dataset entirely and are not on the map: KK Kubang Kual (Pasir Mas), KK Lojing (Gua Musang), KK Panji (Kota Bharu), KK Wakaf Che Yeh (Kota Bharu). No coordinates were invented for them.
  • Only Klinik Kesihatan were validated. Hospitals, Klinik Desa and dental clinics were not checked against official lists.

Limitations

  • Observed extent is not hazard probability. It shows where Sentinel-1 detected water during one event; it says nothing about return periods or future floods.
  • SAR misses water under dense vegetation and in built-up areas (radar shadow and layover), and satellite passes can miss the flood peak. Urban facilities are likely under-counted as "inside".
  • Coordinate precision varies (2–7 decimal places in the source; 21 records have ≤3 dp, i.e. roughly 100 m–1 km precision); a facility a few metres from water may be mis-classified. The 500 m flag is more robust than the inside/outside flag.
  • Exposure is not impact: building elevation, access roads and service disruption are not assessed.
  • The facility list is a 31 Dec 2025 snapshot; it does not reflect the facility set operating in 2024.
